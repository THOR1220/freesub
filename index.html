A private, simple place to keep track of your FreeDNS hostnames.

Step 1 — Register a hostname

FreeSub cannot register DNS names itself. Open FreeDNS to choose an available shared domain and register your hostname there.

  1. Open FreeDNS using the button above and sign in.
  2. Use its subdomain/hostname registration page to choose an available domain and name.
  3. Complete the registration on FreeDNS. Keep the full hostname it gives you.
  4. Come back here and add the full hostname below.

Important: registering a hostname and configuring its DNS record are separate tasks. FreeSub does not create DNS records or check whether a hostname is live.

Step 2 — Add it to your dashboard

Enter the full hostname exactly as FreeDNS shows it, for example game.mooo.com. You can save it as pending if you have not registered it yet.
